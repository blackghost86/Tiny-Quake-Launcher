Tiny Quake Launcher
---------------------------------------------------------------------
A simple launcher for Quake and its mission packs.

Features
------------------------------------------------------------
- Runs Quake from your desktop or Quake folder (needs Quake game path).
- Simple and easy to use if you have no idea how to setup Quake.
- Detects add-ons/maps in custom folders and .pak files.

Installation
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Extract TQLauncher.exe on your desktop or into your Quake directory.

Prerequisites
---------------------------------------------------------------------
.net 10.0 Desktop Runtime (https://builds.dotnet.microsoft.com/dotnet/WindowsDesktop/10.0.11/windowsdesktop-runtime-10.0.11-win-x64.exe).
